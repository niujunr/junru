Outreach email agent

Running international outreach from plain-language requests in Codex: sending and reading Feishu mail, tidying the inbox, tracking who replied, and finding new contacts.

International outreach means keeping track of thousands of addresses and institutions: who has been emailed, who replied, who pointed me to a colleague, and which mail can be cleared out. By hand, that gets messy fast. Now I state the goal, Codex runs local scripts in a fixed workflow, and everything is kept in local ledgers so the next session picks up where the last one stopped.

Just ask

  • Where do things stand — who replied and who didn’t?
  • Send this sheet, but send me a test copy first
  • Tidy up the inbox and show me what can go
  • Find contacts at premium members’ clubs in the Middle East
  • We’ve connected with this institution, so stop emailing them

Workflow

  1. List or search brief
  2. Extraction and discovery
  3. Quality filter
  4. Duplicate check
  5. Preview, then confirm and send
  6. Sync sent mail and inbox
  7. Contact, institution and follow-up ledgers

Guardrails

  • Every send shows the full subject, recipients and body first and goes out only after I confirm, with at most 30 BCC recipients per batch.
  • Inbox cleanup only touches safe categories such as auto-replies, bounces and my own copies; real replies are always kept.
  • Contact discovery uses official, public sources only, with no open-ended crawling. New addresses go into a local candidate pool and are checked for duplicates before anything can be sent.
