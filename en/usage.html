About the data

  • ChatGPT: official lifetime and daily token counts, read from my OpenAI account every day. This includes tasks run in the cloud and on other devices.
  • Claude: read from the conversation records the Claude app keeps on this computer. Every reply records its token count; this sums input, output, and cache reads and writes. Chats in the web and mobile apps aren’t stored locally, so they’re not included.
  • Most tokens are context that gets resent with every turn of a conversation, so they don’t equal how much was written.
  • Grok: Grok keeps no local records. These numbers come from the usage export in the xAI console (covering Grok Bot, Grok chat and Grok Tasks) and are counted by day in the calendar and charts. I import the export by hand.
  • Numbers already recorded never go down when old conversations are deleted.
